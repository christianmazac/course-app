# Listen with curiosity

Listening to understand is different from waiting for your turn to speak. A little curiosity can help both people feel clearer, even when you do not agree.

## Practice the listening loop

1. Give the speaker your attention for a few minutes.
2. Reflect the main point in your own words: “It sounds like…”
3. Ask one open question: “What part feels most important to you?”
4. Check your understanding before offering ideas.

You do not need to solve the situation. Ask whether the person wants listening, brainstorming, or practical help.

## Try it in a low-stakes conversation

Afterwards, notice which question helped you understand more. Listening can include a respectful boundary if you need to pause or return to the conversation later.
