# Say what you need with care

A boundary communicates what you can do, what you cannot do, or what you need next. It is clearer when it describes your own action rather than trying to control another person.

## Use a simple structure

“I can [what is possible]. I cannot [limit]. I can [alternative or next step].”

For example: “I can review one section today. I cannot finish the full report by noon. I can send notes on the opening section this afternoon.”

## Practice

Write a boundary for a real, low-stakes situation. Keep it brief, respectful, and specific. You can be kind and still be clear; a boundary does not require a long defense.

If safety is a concern, prioritize support and a safe plan over having a direct conversation.
