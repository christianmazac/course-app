# Set a goal that matters to you

A useful goal connects an outcome you care about to a next action you can influence. It should guide your choices without turning your worth into a score.

## Draft a grounded goal

- **Why it matters to me:** Name the value or need underneath the goal.
- **What I can influence:** Choose a behavior, not another person's response.
- **The next small action:** Make it possible to begin this week.
- **How I will review it:** Pick a date to learn and adjust.

“Become more successful” is hard to act on. “Spend 20 minutes on my portfolio on Tuesday” is testable and adjustable.

## Leave room for life

Choose a minimum version for busy days and a sign that tells you the plan needs changing. Changing the plan is part of learning.
