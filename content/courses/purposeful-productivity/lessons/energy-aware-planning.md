# Plan around your energy

Time estimates matter, but energy and context matter too. A plan is easier to follow when demanding work has space and routine tasks have a place.

## Make a simple energy map

For a few days, note when you tend to feel most alert, steady, or low-energy. There is no ideal pattern. Use your own observations rather than comparing your schedule with someone else's.

Place one important, demanding task in a period when you are usually more alert. Batch or simplify routine work where it fits. Leave small buffers around transitions.

## Adjust with care

If your energy changes, reduce the task, move it, or ask for help. A sustainable plan includes breaks and recovery; it is not a test of how much you can push through.
