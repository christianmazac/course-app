# Run a ten-minute weekly reset

A weekly reset is a short review that helps you close open loops and choose a manageable next step. It is not a demand to optimize every hour.

## Ten-minute review

1. **Notice:** What moved forward, even a little?
2. **Learn:** What felt easier or harder than expected?
3. **Release:** What can be paused, delegated, or removed?
4. **Choose:** What are one to three priorities for the coming week?
5. **Prepare:** What is the first small action, and when will you start?

Keep the notes somewhere easy to revisit. If a plan repeatedly does not fit, make it smaller or change the conditions instead of blaming yourself.
