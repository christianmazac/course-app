# Design a one-week experiment

A goal can feel too large to begin. An experiment is smaller: it lets you learn what works without demanding a permanent promise.

## Make your experiment

- **I want to learn:** What makes it easier for me to take a short walk?
- **For one week, I will:** Walk for ten minutes after lunch on two days.
- **I will make it easier by:** Putting my shoes by the door.
- **I will notice:** My energy before and after, without judging the result.

Choose your own question and a version you can repeat at least once. If the week becomes busy, reduce the experiment rather than treating a missed day as failure.

## Close the loop

At the end of the week, ask: What helped? What got in the way? What is one adjustment I want to try next?
