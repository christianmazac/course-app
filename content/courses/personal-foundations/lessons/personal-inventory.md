# Take a strengths-first inventory

Self-improvement works best when it starts with what is already working, not with a verdict about what is wrong. A strengths-first inventory helps you see patterns you can build on.

## Try it

Write down three moments from the last month when you felt capable, engaged, or useful. For each one, note:

- What were you doing?
- Which strength did you use (for example, patience, curiosity, or follow-through)?
- What conditions helped that strength show up?

Look for a pattern across the moments. A strength is not a fixed label or a reason to do everything the same way; it is a resource you can choose to use.

## Small experiment

Choose one strength and use it deliberately in a situation this week. Afterwards, note what felt helpful and what you would change.
