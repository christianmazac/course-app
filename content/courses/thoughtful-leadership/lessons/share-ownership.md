# Share ownership, not just tasks

Delegating well means sharing context and decision space, not simply transferring a checklist. People need enough clarity to act and enough trust to choose how.

## Set up shared ownership

Explain the outcome and why it matters. Agree on boundaries, resources, and a check-in point. Ask what support would help, then let the person choose a reasonable approach.

Avoid taking the work back at the first difference in style. If a risk appears, discuss it early and adjust together. Feedback should clarify the work, not remove the other person's agency.

## Practice

Choose a small task you can share this week. Name what success looks like, what decisions the other person owns, and when you will reconnect.
