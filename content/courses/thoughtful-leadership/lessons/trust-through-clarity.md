# Build trust through clear agreements

Trust grows through everyday experiences of being heard, knowing what to expect, and seeing people follow through. Clear agreements reduce the guesswork for everyone.

## Make an agreement visible

For a shared task, clarify:

- What outcome are we aiming for?
- Who owns the next step?
- When will we check in?
- What should happen if the plan changes?

Invite questions and make it safe to raise uncertainty early. An agreement is not a contract for perfection; it is a shared starting point that people can update.

## Reflect

Think of one recurring handoff that could become clearer. Ask the people involved what information would help them do their best work.
