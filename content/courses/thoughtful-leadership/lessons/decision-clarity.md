# Make the decision and owner clear

Teams lose time when a discussion ends without agreement about who decides, what was decided, or when to revisit it. A short decision note gives people a common reference.

## Capture four things

- **Decision:** What are we choosing for now?
- **Owner:** Who will take the next action?
- **Reason:** What information or trade-off mattered most?
- **Review point:** What would make us revisit the decision?

Not every decision needs consensus. Be explicit about who has input and who is accountable, and make space for concerns before closing the discussion.

## Try it

After your next team decision, write a three-sentence summary and invite corrections. Clarity is a practice, not a claim that everyone agrees.
