# Name the feeling without judgment

Strong feelings can narrow attention. Pausing to describe what is happening may create room for a considered response. It does not make a difficult situation disappear.

## A one-minute check-in

- **Notice:** What sensations or impulses are present?
- **Name:** What emotion might fit? More than one can be true.
- **Allow:** “I am noticing ___ right now.” This is a description, not an instruction.
- **Choose:** What is one kind, safe next step?

Use plain language and avoid grading the feeling as good or bad. If naming it is hard, describe the body sensation or write “uncertain.”

## Keep support in reach

These reflection exercises are educational, not mental-health treatment. If distress feels overwhelming or you feel unsafe, reach out to someone you trust or a qualified local support service.
