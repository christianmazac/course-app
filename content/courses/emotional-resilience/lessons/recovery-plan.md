# Create a personal recovery plan

Recovery does not have to mean bouncing back instantly. A plan can help you find one supportive next step when you have less capacity.

## Fill in your plan

- **Early signs I need a pause:** (for example, rushing or skipping meals)
- **A short reset that is usually safe for me:** (water, fresh air, a quiet moment)
- **A person or resource I can contact:** (choose someone you trust)
- **One thing that can wait:** (give yourself permission to defer it)

Keep the plan realistic and easy to find. Your needs can change, so revisit it when you have more space.

## Remember

Rest and asking for help are valid actions. If you are in immediate danger or cannot keep yourself safe, contact emergency services or crisis support available where you live.
